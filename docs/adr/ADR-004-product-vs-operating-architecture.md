# ADR-004: Product Architecture vs Business Operating Architecture

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-10

## Context

High Kode already operates products including DingDongBot and AIWeb.

These products have their own product architecture, backend systems,
databases, customers, and product-specific logic.

At the same time, High Kode is building an internal AI-first business
operating system.

These are different architectural concerns.

## Decision

Product Architecture and Business Operating Architecture are separated.

### Product Architecture

DingDongBot and AIWeb remain independently architected products.

They are not generated by `high_kode_ai`.

### Business Operating Architecture

High Kode's internal operations are managed through:

- ERPNext
- `high_kode_integrations`
- `high_kode_ai`

Existing products may connect to this operating architecture through the
integration layer.
